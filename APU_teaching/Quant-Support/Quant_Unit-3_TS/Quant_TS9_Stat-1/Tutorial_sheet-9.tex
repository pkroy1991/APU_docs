\item The blood groups of 30 students of Class IX are recorded as follows:
\begin{align*}
A, B, O, O, AB, O, A, O, B, A, O, B, A, O, O, \\
A, AB, O, A, A, O, O, AB, B, A, O, B, A, B, O.
\end{align*}
Represent this data in the form of a frequency distribution table. Which is the most
common, and which is the rarest, blood group among these students?

\item The heights of 50 students, measured to the nearest centimetres, have been found to
be as follows:
\begin{align*}
161 \quad 150 \quad 154 \quad 165 \quad 168 \quad161 \quad 154 \quad 162 \quad 150 \quad 151 \\
162 \quad 164 \quad 171 \quad 165 \quad 158 \quad 154 \quad 156 \quad 172 \quad 160 \quad 170 \\

154 \quad 152 \quad 153 \quad 156 \quad 158 \quad 162 \quad 160 \quad 161 \quad 173 \quad 166 \\
161 \quad 159 \quad 162 \quad 167 \quad 168 \quad 159 \quad 158 \quad 153 \quad 154 \quad 159

\end{align*}
Prepare a frequency distribution table for the data given above.

\item The value of $\pi$ upto 50 decimal places is given below:
\[
3.14159265358979323846264338327950288419716939937510

 \item What are the most and the least frequently occurring digits?
\end{enumerate}
